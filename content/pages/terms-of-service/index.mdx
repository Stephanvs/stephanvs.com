## About Popcorn

Popcorn is an independent iPhone and iPad app developed by Stephan van Stekelenburg in the Netherlands. It connects to a TeamCity server you choose so that you can view projects and builds and perform build actions.

Popcorn is not affiliated with or endorsed by JetBrains. TeamCity is a trademark of JetBrains s.r.o.

## Your TeamCity connection

You must have authorized access to an existing TeamCity Cloud instance. Popcorn does not provide a TeamCity account, server, build agent, or TeamCity license. The terms of your TeamCity service and organization apply separately.

Use a personal access token with appropriate permissions and protect it as a credential. Only connect to servers and access projects you are authorized to use. You can remove a saved connection in Popcorn Settings and revoke its token in TeamCity.

## Build actions

Starting or cancelling a build sends a request to your configured server. These actions can affect your team's work and use server or build-agent resources. Check the selected server, configuration, and branch before performing an action.

Popcorn's functionality depends on your server's availability, network connectivity, compatible API behavior, and account permissions. The current version checks status while relevant screens are open; it does not provide push notifications or continuous background monitoring.
