## What you can do

- **See every build.** Browse projects and configurations, and see recent builds across branches with status and progress. A chart of the last 20 builds splits run time from queue time, so slow queues stand out.
- **Read live logs.** Follow output as it arrives, search it, and switch line wrapping.
- **Find out why it failed.** Build problems and failed tests, new failures first, with error messages and stack traces when your server has them.
- **Start, re-run or cancel.** Trigger a build on any branch, run it with custom parameters or a specific agent, re-run the same revision, or cancel a queued or running build.
- **Watch your agents.** Agents grouped by pool, with busy, idle and offline filters.
- **Follow your investigations.** The investigations assigned to you, grouped by project.
- **Glance at the Home Screen.** A widget shows one configuration's latest build.

## Your server, your token

Popcorn works with TeamCity Cloud and with self-hosted TeamCity over HTTPS. You sign in with a personal access token, which Popcorn keeps in the iOS Keychain. Requests go straight from your device to your server. There is no Popcorn account, no Popcorn server, and no analytics or advertising code in the app.

## Questions

### Does it work with self-hosted TeamCity?

Yes, as long as the server uses HTTPS with a valid certificate. For a server on a private network, connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi or VPN first.

### What permissions does the token need?

Read access is enough to view builds, logs and agents. Starting and cancelling builds needs the matching TeamCity permissions. Read [how to create a read-only TeamCity token](/blog/teamcity-read-only-token/) for a safe first setup.

## Connect to TeamCity

1. Open your TeamCity server in a browser and sign in.
2. Create a personal access token in your TeamCity profile with the permissions you need. Your TeamCity administrator may need to enable access or grant permissions.
3. For TeamCity Cloud, enter your instance name, for example `my-team` for `https://my-team.teamcity.com`. HTTPS and `.teamcity.com` are added automatically. For a self-hosted server, enter its HTTPS URL.
4. Enter your token and connect. Tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain and sent directly to your TeamCity server.

You need an existing TeamCity Cloud account or a self-hosted TeamCity server over HTTPS, an internet connection, and iOS 17 or later. Plain HTTP is not supported.

### I cannot connect

Check that your server opens in a browser over HTTPS, that the address is correct, and that your personal access token has not expired or been revoked. For TeamCity Cloud, enter just the part before `.teamcity.com`. Confirm your token's permissions with your administrator.

### A build action is unavailable

Starting and cancelling builds requires the corresponding TeamCity permissions. Agent status and investigations are read-only in Popcorn.

### How do I remove a connection?

Open Settings and remove the saved server. This deletes its profile and Keychain token from this device. Remove each server before uninstalling if you want to clear its credentials: Keychain items can persist after uninstalling. You can also revoke the token in TeamCity.
